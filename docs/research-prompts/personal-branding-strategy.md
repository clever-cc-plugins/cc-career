# Research Prompt: personal-branding-strategy

**For:** `cc-career:personal-branding-strategy`
**Current knowledge base:** `plugins/cc-career/skills/_shared/personal-branding-frameworks.md` —
Simon Sinek's Golden Circle (applied personally) and Personal SWOT, applied
from general knowledge with no dedicated research doc behind them yet.
**Why research helps:** ground "personal branding" — a term with a lot of
low-quality practitioner content — in the more credible research that does
exist, and surface frameworks with actual evidence behind them.

Paste the prompt below into a deep-research AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini,
Perplexity, or similar). Save the response as Markdown, e.g. to
`docs/research/personal-branding-strategy.md`, then use it to refine
`personal-branding-frameworks.md`.

```
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Research prompt: personal branding frameworks and evidence
─────────────────────────────────────────────
You are a research analyst investigating evidence-based approaches to
personal/professional branding for individuals, for use in a coaching/
career-strategy tool. The field has a lot of low-quality "influencer" advice;
prioritize academic and well-evidenced practitioner sources.

Cover, with headers separating each area:

1. Academic research on personal branding as a construct — origin of the
   term (Tom Peters, 1997), and what organizational-behavior/HR research
   since then says about its actual career impact (does a stronger personal
   brand correlate with measurable outcomes — promotions, opportunities,
   perceived expertise — and under what conditions).
2. Simon Sinek's Golden Circle (Why/How/What) applied to individuals rather
   than organizations — how practitioners adapt it, and any critiques of
   over-applying a Why-first framing to career contexts where it doesn't fit.
3. SWOT analysis applied to individuals ("Personal SWOT") — evidence for its
   effectiveness as a self-assessment tool vs. more validated alternatives
   (e.g. structured self-assessment instruments, 360-degree feedback framed
   for individual use).
4. Research on authenticity vs. strategic self-presentation in professional
   contexts — when a curated public message helps vs. backfires (e.g.
   research on impression management, self-verification theory).
5. What distinguishes an effective content/visibility strategy from
   generic "post more" advice — any evidence-based guidance on cadence,
   platform choice, or content-to-authenticity ratio for someone building
   visibility around genuine expertise rather than personal-brand-as-hype.

Require credible, verifiable sources throughout, with in-text citations —
prioritize peer-reviewed research and well-established practitioner sources
over marketing-blog content. Provide a formatted source list in APA style at
the end. Respond in Markdown.
─────────────────────────────────────────────
```
